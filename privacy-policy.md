# Privacy Policy for Hamband

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

## Overview

Hamband is a ham radio app for iPhone and Apple Watch that shows HF band conditions and US amateur band privileges. This privacy policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data Collection

Hamband does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or any personal details to use.

## Data Usage

Hamband fetches publicly available solar weather data from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (swpc.noaa.gov). This request is made directly from your device to NOAA's public API. No data about you or your device is sent to NOAA or any other third party as part of this request.

To keep its band privileges chart current, Hamband also checks the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (ecfr.gov), the official online edition of the federal rules, at most once a day. It asks only when the FCC's amateur radio rules (47 CFR Part 97) were last changed. This request is made directly from your device to eCFR's public API, and no data about you or your device is sent with it.

As with any internet request, these public services can see your device's IP address. Hamband sends nothing else.

## Third Party Services

Hamband does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Storage

Hamband does not store any data on external servers. Solar weather data is fetched in real time from NOAA's public API.

A few settings are saved only on your device so the app remembers them between launches: the license class you select and your display choices (theme, "only bands I can use"), when the app last checked eCFR for rule changes and what it found, and a count of successful launches used to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt. These never leave your device and are deleted if you delete the app.

## Children's Privacy

Hamband does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
