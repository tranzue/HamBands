# Privacy Policy for Hamband

*Last updated: May 23, 2026*

## Overview

Hamband is a ham radio HF band conditions app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This privacy policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data Collection

Hamband does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or any personal details to use.

## Data Usage

Hamband fetches publicly available solar weather data from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (swpc.noaa.gov). This request is made directly from your device to NOAA's public API. No data about you or your device is sent to NOAA or any other third party as part of this request.

## Third Party Services

Hamband does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Storage

Hamband does not store any data on external servers. All data displayed in the app is fetched in real time from NOAA's public API and exists only temporarily on your device.

## Children's Privacy

Hamband does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

If this policy changes in a future update, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
